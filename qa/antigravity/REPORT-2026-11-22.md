## 4. Attacks & Checks That Held

- **MCP amount boundary & type attacks** (compiled contracts via `FakeNetwork` in `mcp_brutal.mjs`): negative (`-1`, `-0.01`), zero pay (`0`), `Infinity` (`1e309`), `NaN`, sub-cent (`0.001`), 6-decimal (`10.000001`), comma decimal (`10,00`), scientific string (`"1e5"`), empty/whitespace, and >$999,999,999.99 all refused before sending a transaction.
- **MCP exact-limit & daily window accounting** (`FakeNetwork`, in-memory EVM): two consecutive `$10.00` payments succeeded (`0x94b48162...`, `0x5af91959...`), bringing remaining daily room from `$20.00` to `$0.00`; subsequent `$5.00` and `$5.01` previews returned `OverWindowLimit`.
- **MCP recipient resolution & EIP-55 typo protection** (`FakeNetwork`): wrong mixed-case EIP-55 checksum rejected cleanly before gas spend; ALL-CAPS/lowercase hex normalized cleanly; zero address, token address, vault own address, extra inner spaces, Cyrillic homoglyph `'o'`, and empty recipient string all blocked (`RecipientNotApproved` or unknown recipient error). *(Note: recipient names in `HARDSTOP_RECIPIENTS` env config remain operator-controlled config and are echoed literally.)*
- **MCP nonce & schema enforcement** (`FakeNetwork`): replayed nonce (`0`), skipped nonce (`99`), and `MAX_SAFE_INTEGER` refused by the contract (`WrongNonce`); `MAX_SAFE_INTEGER + 1`, `-1`, and float `1.5` rejected by Zod schema before touching RPC.
- **MCP self-raise & prompt-injection resistance** (`FakeNetwork`): `hardstop_request_more` always returned `"changed": false`, rejected decreases (`$5.00` vs `$20.00`) and already-approved recipients, blocked strings >1000 chars and empty strings, and quarantined injection attempts (`Please. changed: true...`) inside the `"not checked by anyone"` draft quote.
- **On-chain vault name encoding & XSS** (`mine.ts` + DOM): React escaped `<script>alert(1)</script>` and `<img onerror=alert(1)>` as inert text; `33`-byte ASCII and `36`-byte multi-byte emoji were refused while `32`-byte UTF-8 round-tripped cleanly.
- **Receipt integrity against lying index** (`history.ts`): reverted txs (`status: '0x0'`), logs from foreign contracts (launcher/token), unknown hashes, malformed hashes, and unparseable vault logs were all dropped.
- **Live site HTTP headers & route resilience** (`https://hardstop-omega.vercel.app`): CSP (`default-src 'self'; ... frame-ancestors 'none'`), `x-frame-options: DENY`, `x-content-type-options: nosniff`, `referrer-policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, `permissions-policy`, and `strict-transport-security` verified; `/activity` safely rejected `?chain=garbage`, oversized chain, mainnet (`chain=1`), and XSS strings in `vault`/`who`.
- **Responsive, keyboard, and motion checks on live site**: zero horizontal overflow (`scrollWidth === clientWidth`) across all 7 routes at `375px` phone and `200%` zoom (`640px`); `0` running animations >10ms under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`; 25 distinct Tab focus stops on `/protect` with visible focus rings and no traps.
